## Proxy Winner DNA

The strongest reusable pattern is not “add more AI.” It is:

`precise real problem → real FortyGuard local data → decision/action → low-friction delivery → credible human/business impact → clear presentation`

Derived laws:

1. **Temperature must change an action.** A heatmap alone is weaker than a changed route, schedule, allocation, alert, or operating decision.
2. **Real local data matters.** FortyGuard explicitly celebrated Care Cast for being grounded in location-specific data.
3. **The beneficiary/operator must be concrete.** Generic “cities” or “users” are weaker than a named role with an operational job.
4. **Accessibility / execution simplicity can beat feature count.** Care Cast reduced interface friction rather than adding complexity.
5. **Impact must survive scrutiny.** The current FortyGuard judges publicly emphasize meaningful real-world problems and lasting/real-world impact.
6. **Communication is part of the product.** Care Cast's team and mentor highlighted presentation quality; our demo must make the causal decision delta obvious in seconds.
